How it works

  1. Install Sweepkit and open youtube.com/playlist?list=WL (your Watch Later), Liked videos, a playlist or your History.
  2. Click the Sweepkit icon in the toolbar. It shows how many videos it found.
  3. Press Remove. A small panel on the page shows progress, with Pause and Stop.

Questions

Is this safe? Can it get my account in trouble?

Sweepkit only clicks the same Remove buttons you would click yourself, inside your own browser session. It adds pauses between removals and backs off when YouTube rate-limits. We've seen no account issues, but like any automation, use the Careful speed on very large lists.

Why is there a daily limit on the free version?

Twenty-five a day covers most tidy-ups for free. People with thousands of videos pay once for Pro, which funds keeping the extension working as YouTube changes.

It stopped partway through. What now?

Reload the page and press Remove again — it picks up where it left off. If YouTube is rejecting removals, wait 20–30 minutes and use Careful speed.

Can I undo?

Removals are the same as removing by hand, so there's no bulk undo. Use the CSV backup option first; it saves every title and link.

Does it work in other languages?

Most YouTube display languages work. If a menu isn't recognised, switch YouTube to English while it runs.
