Privacy Policy

Effective: November 9, 2026

The short version: Sweepkit processes your YouTube lists in your browser and does not automatically send those lists to Sweepkit. The extension has no analytics. Settings and licensing records are stored locally; optional Pro licensing contacts Polar, and optional diagnostics are copied only when you request them.

What the extension does

When you click the Sweepkit icon on a YouTube list page (Watch Later, Liked videos, a playlist, watch history or subscriptions) and press Remove, the extension clicks YouTube's own "Remove" controls on that page on your behalf. It reads the titles, channel names, video links, durations, watch-progress bars, upload ages and deleted/private status shown on the page only to display progress, apply the filters you set, and, if you ask for it, save a CSV backup to your computer.

Data we collect

The extension does not automatically send browsing history, YouTube account information, video lists, or usage statistics to Sweepkit. You may choose to send information to support; review what you share before sending it.

Data stored on your device

  • Your settings (including filters, cleanup rules and your keep-channel list), the install date, the count of removals made today and whether the next run should go slower after YouTube rate-limited it, kept in the extension's local storage in your browser.
  • If you buy Pro: your license key, activation ID, license status, and activation, expiry and validation dates, kept in the same local storage so the extension knows it's unlocked.

Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension storage. CSV files or diagnostics you separately save, copy or send are not removed by uninstalling.

Pro licensing

Purchases are processed by our merchant of record (Polar Software Inc.), which handles payment and tax and has its own privacy policy. Activating Pro sends your license key, the configured Sweepkit organization ID and an activation label containing Chrome, platform and date to Polar. Validation and deactivation also send the activation ID when one exists. The extension may revalidate a saved key when licensing is checked more than seven days after the last validation. No YouTube list data is included in these licensing requests.

Optional support diagnostics

Copy diagnostics places a report on your clipboard only when you click it. The report contains the extension version, list type, row and button counts, and a fixed run status or error code. It excludes page URLs, playlist and video names, channel names, DOM text and browser identifiers. It is not automatically sent anywhere. You choose whether to send it to support.

Permissions explained

  • activeTab / scripting — lets Sweepkit inject its packaged code into the active YouTube tab after your interaction. The extension limits its supported workflows to YouTube list pages; it does not request persistent access to all websites.
  • storage — saves your settings and daily count locally.
  • api.polar.sh — used for Pro license activation, validation and deactivation.

Children

Sweepkit is not directed at children under 13. Its local processing, licensing and optional support behavior are described above.
