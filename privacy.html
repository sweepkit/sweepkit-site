Privacy Policy

Effective: October 26, 2026

The short version: Sweepkit runs entirely in your browser. It does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. It has no servers and no analytics.

What the extension does

When you click the Sweepkit icon on a YouTube list page (Watch Later, Liked videos, a playlist, watch history or subscriptions) and press Remove, the extension clicks YouTube's own "Remove" controls on that page on your behalf. It reads the titles, channel names and durations shown on the page only to display progress, apply the filters you set, and, if you ask for it, save a CSV backup to your computer.

Data we collect

None. Sweepkit does not collect browsing history, YouTube account information, video lists, or usage statistics. Nothing is sent to us.

Data stored on your device

  • Your settings (speed, filter text) and the count of removals made today, kept in the extension's local storage in your browser.
  • If you buy Pro: your license key and an activation ID, kept in the same local storage so the extension knows it's unlocked.

Uninstalling the extension deletes all of this.

Pro licensing

Purchases are processed by our merchant of record (Polar Software Inc.), which handles payment and tax and has its own privacy policy. When you paste a license key into Sweepkit, the key is sent to the licensing service to confirm it's valid. No YouTube data is included in that request.

Permissions explained

  • activeTab / scripting — lets Sweepkit run on the YouTube tab you clicked it on, and only after you click. It cannot read other tabs or sites.
  • storage — saves your settings and daily count locally.
  • api.polar.sh — used only to verify a Pro license key you enter.

Children

Sweepkit is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
