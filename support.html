Support

"Nothing to remove here" / the button is disabled

Make sure you're on one of these pages: youtube.com/playlist?list=WL (Watch Later), youtube.com/playlist?list=LL (Liked videos), a playlist URL, youtube.com/feed/history, or youtube.com/feed/channels. Reload the page, then click the Sweepkit icon again.

It stopped after a while

YouTube sometimes rejects rapid removals. Sweepkit waits and retries automatically; if it gives up, wait 20–30 minutes, reload, choose Careful speed and press Remove again. It continues from where it stopped.

The menu isn't recognised (non-English YouTube)

Switch YouTube's display language to English (profile picture → Language) while Sweepkit runs, then switch back. Tell us your language and we'll add it.

Where's my CSV backup?

In your browser's Downloads folder, named sweepkit-watch-later-YYYY-MM-DD.csv.

I bought Pro — how do I activate?

Your license key is in the purchase email. Click the Sweepkit icon → I already have a license key → paste → Activate. Pro works on up to 3 browsers; deactivate from the popup to free a slot.

Refunds

If Pro doesn't work for you, email us within 14 days of purchase and we'll refund it, no questions asked.

Can I undo removals?

No — removals are the same as doing them by hand. Use the CSV backup option before running.

Still stuck? Email support with the page you were on and what the popup said. Replies usually within a day.
